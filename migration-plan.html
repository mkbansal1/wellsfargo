1. Executive Summary

  • 1,281 sitemap URLs total: 907 English + 374 Spanish. Of the 907 English pages, 644 are in scope across 14 templates. 263 English pages + 374 Spanish pages are scoped out (see Section 2 breakdown).
  • 6 blocks already in the repo: hero, cards, columns, fragment, header, footer
  • 4 blocks from Adobe Block Collection: accordion, tabs, carousel, table
  • 7 custom blocks required: promo-bar, rate-checker, large-promo, app-promo, contact-bar, product-comparison, icon-cards
  • Proprietary font "Wells Fargo Sans Regular" requires licensing decision before design Phase 1
  • Mega-menu with 6 top-level groups and nested product links requires custom header decoration
  • Interactive widgets (rate checker, location finder, chatbot, calculators) are out of scope — replaced with static CTAs
  • 637 pages scoped out: 374 Spanish (/es/), 255 private foundations (repetitive grant pages), 3 nav links, 3 test pages, 2 jump/redirect pages. Auth flows, commerce, MarTech also deferred.
  • Design palette: brand red (#D71E28), yellow (#FCC60A), navy (#27455C), warm grays, purple links (#5A469B)
  • Estimated effort: 3 phases — infrastructure + design (~5 sessions), block dev (~5 sessions), bulk import + QA (~3 sessions)

Brand Decisions Required

  • Font licensing: "Wells Fargo Sans Regular" is proprietary. Options: (a) license and self-host via fonts/ + fonts.css, or (b) fall back to Arial/Helvetica system stack.

10. Open Questions

  1. Font licensing: Can "Wells Fargo Sans Regular" be self-hosted on the EDS domain? If not, should we use Arial or another approved fallback?
  2. Scope confirmation: Are Business (/biz/), Commercial (/com/), and CIB (/cib/) pages in scope, or consumer-only?
  3. Promotional content cadence: How often do hero and promo card offers rotate? Should sections be templatized for easier author updates?
  4. Rate checker: Should the EDS version be (a) a static link to the rates page, (b) a custom block fetching from an API, or (c) removed entirely?
  5. Locale: Is Spanish (/es/) a future phase? Timeline expectations?
  6. Analytics / MarTech: Which tracking scripts need to go in delayed.js? Can the team provide tag manager configuration?
  7. Search: Should we implement the EDS Search block or redirect to existing WF search infrastructure?
  8. Cookie consent / privacy: Which compliance framework (OneTrust, etc.) should be integrated?
  9. Sign On: Keep as external link to connect.secure.wellsfargo.com, or is there a plan to integrate authentication?
  10. Video content (CIB): Host videos on EDS media or embed from external source (YouTube, Brightcove, etc.)?
